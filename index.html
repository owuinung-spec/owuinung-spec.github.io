Everything the counter needs

Built for owner-run shops. The tools you use every day are free; the deeper reports and staff tools are on the Paid plan.

  • Billing Free

    Ring up a sale, take cash, UPI, card or credit, add a discount, then print or share the receipt with your own logo on it.

  • Stock Free up to 10 products

    Products with variants, barcode scanning, purchase invoices, and a history of every stock change so counts can be traced.

  • Low-stock alerts Paid

    See what is running out before a customer asks for it.

  • Sales and profit reports Paid

    Sales, cost, profit and expenses by day, week or month. Best sellers, thin margins, and month-to-month growth. The Free plan shows your last 7 days of sales.

  • Forecast and restock planner Paid

    An estimate of the next weeks, with a range and a plain note on how much to trust it, and a list of what to reorder.

  • Staff with limits you set Paid

    Add managers and cashiers. Choose what each person can open. Keep cost price and profit private to the people you pick.

  • More than one shop Paid

    Run a second business under the same login, with its own stock, bills and staff.

  • Your data, exportable Paid

    Download your sales, products and customers as spreadsheets whenever you want.

Made for the way a small shop runs

Owi Compass started in Tuensang, Nagaland, for shops where the owner is also the cashier, the buyer and the bookkeeper.

  • Cost and profit stay privateStaff can bill customers all day without ever seeing what you paid for anything.
  • One account, two ways inSign in with your email and password, or with Google. Use the same email and you land in the same shop.
  • No ads, no trackingWe do not show ads or sell your data. The app has no analytics or advertising tools inside it.
  • Pay the simple wayPaid plans are paid by UPI. We confirm your payment by hand, so there is no card to store and no auto-renewal to cancel.
