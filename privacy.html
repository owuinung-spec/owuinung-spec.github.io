Privacy policy

What Owi Compass collects, why, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do.

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

Owi Compass is an Android app for small shops, made by Owuinüng in Tuensang, Nagaland, India. In this policy, "we" means Owuinüng. You can reach us at .

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we need it
Your account. Email address and name. If you use Google sign-in, Google gives us your name and email. We never see your Google password.To create your account and sign you in.
Your business details. Business name, phone, address, type, logo, tax settings, and the name, phone, email, role and permissions of staff you add.To run your shop in the app and print your receipts.
Your shop records. Products, prices, cost prices, stock, purchases, bills and what was sold, discounts, payment modes, returns, expenses, and the customer names and phone numbers you choose to save.This is the service itself: billing, stock tracking and reports.
Plan and payment details. Your plan, how long it runs, and the payment reference you send us.To switch your plan on and keep our records.
Support messages. What you write to us through the app or by email.To answer you and fix problems.
A usage summary. When your shop was last active, number of bills and products, the total of your sales over the last 30 days, how many items are low in stock, the app version, and the name and role of the last person who used the app.To give support, manage plans, and decide when an old app version must be updated.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your messages, or your advertising ID. We do not show ads. The app contains no advertising or analytics tools.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and to take product or logo pictures. A picture is stored only if you choose to attach it.
  • Photos and media: to choose a logo or product picture from your phone.
  • Notifications and vibration: to alert you in the app.
  • Internet and network state: to save your records and to know whether you are online.

Where your information is kept

Your records are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which acts as our service provider. The database servers are in Singapore. Information travels between the app and the servers over encrypted connections.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app file is hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics tools.

Who can see your information

  • You, and the staff you add, limited to what you allow each person to open.
  • Owuinüng. Our administrator account can read business records. We use that access only to give you support you asked for, to manage plans and payments, to keep the service running, and where the law requires.
  • Google, as our hosting and sign-in provider.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose information if a law or a court order requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your business records within 30 days. We may keep payment records for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records. Copies in Google's systems are removed on Google's normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your information inside the app, or ask us to.
  • Export your sales, products and customers from Settings, on the Paid plan.
  • Ask us to delete your account. Steps are on the delete your account page.
  • Ask a question or make a complaint about how we handle your information. We aim to answer within 7 days.

Keeping your account safe

Firebase access rules limit each business's records to the people on that business. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password, confirm your email, and add staff only with the permissions they need. You are responsible for who you give access to.

Customers' details you enter

If you save your customers' names and phone numbers, you decide what to record and why. Please keep only what you need and use it only for your own shop's business.

Children

Owi Compass is made for business owners and their staff. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect children's information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
