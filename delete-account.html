Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Owi Compass account and the records stored with it.

How to ask

  1. Write to from the email address you sign in with. The subject line is already filled in for you.
  2. Tell us your business name, and whether you want to delete one business or the whole account.
  3. We reply to confirm it is you. We may ask you to confirm something only you would know about the account.
  4. We delete the records within 30 days of confirming, and tell you when it is done.

Deleting cannot be undone. Before you ask, export what you want to keep. On the Paid plan, open Settings, then Export.

What we delete

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • The business or businesses you choose, with their products, stock history, bills, returns, expenses, customers and staff access.
  • Your support messages.

What we may keep

We may keep payment records for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records. These do not include your shop's sales or customer records.

If you are a staff member

Staff accounts belong to the shop owner who added you. Ask the owner to remove your access, or write to us and we will pass your request on.

For more about what we store, read the privacy policy.
