Help and support

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, write to us and we will reply.

Contact us

Tell us your shop name, the email you sign in with, and what happened. A screenshot helps. We aim to reply within 7 days. You can also use Support inside the app.

Common questions

How do I install or update the app?

Go to the download page, tap Download the APK, and open the file. For an update, install the new file over the old one. Your data stays in your account.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password. We send a reset link to that address. Check your spam folder if you do not see it.

The confirmation email did not arrive.

Look in your spam or junk folder first. On the Confirm your email screen, tap Send the email again. It can take a few minutes to arrive.

Can I sign in with both email and Google?

Yes, if you use the same email address for both and your email is confirmed. Both then open the same account and the same shops.

What happens when my internet drops?

The app keeps showing your saved data. Selling while offline works only when one person sells for your business and the sales history shows only that person. If more than one person can sell, selling pauses until you are back online, so two phones can never sell the same last item. Sales made offline sync when the connection returns.

How do I pay for the Paid plan?

Open Subscription in the app, pay by UPI to the details shown, and send us the payment reference. We confirm it and switch on your plan, usually the same day.

How do I stop staff seeing cost and profit?

Open Staff, edit the person, and turn off See cost, profit & loss. They can still bill customers, but cost price and profit are hidden from them everywhere in the app.

How do I export my data?

On the Paid plan, open Settings, then Export, and choose sales, products or customers. You get a spreadsheet file.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on the delete your account page.
